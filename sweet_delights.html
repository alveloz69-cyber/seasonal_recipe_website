CRISP & SWEET

Caramel Apple Pretzel Bark

A quick no-bake treat sheet made from melted milk chocolate loaded with broken pretzels, diced fresh apples, and caramel drizzle.

⏱ 20 minutes prep + chilling

View Recipe

Ingredients

Ingredients:
  • 12 ounces milk chocolate chips
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 1/4 cups crushed mini pretzels
  • 1 large Granny Smith apple, diced
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 2/3 cup thick caramel sauce
  • Sea salt flakes

Instructions

  1. Line a tray with parchment paper. Toss the diced apples in lemon juice and pat them totally dry with paper towels.
  2. Melt the chocolate chips and oil together in short microwave increments until completely smooth.
  3. Spread the liquid chocolate onto the parchment paper sheet. Scatter the crushed pretzels and dry apple bits across the top, pressing gently.
  4. Drizzle the caramel sauce across the surface and add a light sprinkle of sea salt.
  5. Refrigerate for 2 to 3 hours until firm, then break into shards to serve.
CREAMY DELIGHT

No-Bake Pumpkin Pie Pudding Cups

Individual layered desserts featuring spiced pumpkin custard, crushed gingersnap cookies, cookie butter, and whipped cream.

⏱ 30 minutes prep + chilling

View Recipe

Ingredients

Components:
  • 4 egg yolks and 6 tablespoons sugar
  • 1/4 cup cornstarch, salt, heavy cream, and whole milk
  • 15 oz canned pumpkin purée and pumpkin spices
  • Vanilla extract and butter
  • Gingersnap cookie crumbs, melted cookie butter, and whipped cream

Instructions

  1. Whisk egg yolks, sugar, cornstarch, salt, pumpkin, and spices in a pan. Pour in cream and milk.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring continuously until thickened into pudding. Remove from heat, stir in butter and vanilla, and chill in the fridge for 4 hours.
  3. Fold whipped cream into the chilled pumpkin pudding. Layer inside glasses with crushed gingersnaps and melted cookie butter, topping with extra whipped cream.
QUICK & EASY

Maple Walnut Energy Bites

Nutritious no-bake snack bites blended from raw walnuts, rolled oats, Medjool dates, and real maple syrup.

⏱ 20 minutes total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Ingredients:
  • 2 1/4 cups walnuts (divided)
  • 1 cup old-fashioned oats
  • 1/2 cup pitted Medjool dates
  • 3 tablespoons pure maple syrup
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Finely chop a small portion of the walnuts and reserve them on a plate for outer coating.
  2. Process the remaining walnuts, oats, dates, maple syrup, vanilla, and salt in a food processor until a sticky dough forms.
  3. Roll the mixture into uniform bite-sized spheres, then roll each sphere in the chopped walnuts before chilling.
NO-BAKE CLASSIC

No-Bake Cappuccino Cream Dessert

An icebox dessert built with alternating layers of graham crackers, coffee-chocolate cream cheese pudding, and whipped cream.

⏱ 20 minutes prep + chilling

View Recipe

Ingredients

Ingredients:
  • 15 cinnamon graham crackers
  • 2 packages softened cream cheese
  • 3 packages instant chocolate pudding mix
  • Instant coffee granules, brewed coffee, and milk
  • Ground cinnamon, whipped cream, and chocolate sprinkles

Instructions

  1. Beat the cream cheese until smooth. In a separate bowl, whisk the pudding mix, milk, brewed coffee, coffee granules, and cinnamon, then combine with the cream cheese.
  2. Layer a baking dish with graham crackers, pudding cream, and whipped cream, repeating the pattern multiple times.
  3. Dust with cinnamon and sprinkles, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours to set before serving.
