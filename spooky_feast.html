PUMPKIN MAGIC

Jack-o'-Lantern Cupcakes

Soft pumpkin cupcakes topped with orange frosting and spooky jack-o'-lantern faces.

⏱ 2 hours & 5 minutes total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Cupcakes:
  • 2 cups granulated sugar
  • 2 sticks(16 tablespoons) unsalted butter
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2/3 cups almond flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • Orange frosting and chocolate icing
Frosting:
  • 2 sticks (16 tablespoons) unsalted butter at room temperature
  • 1/4 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 5 tablespoons milk, at room temperature
  • 1 teaspoon orange gel food coloring
Decorations:
  • 6 chewy candy chocolate rolls, cut into quarters
  • 2 green sour gummy belt candies, cut into 1/8-inch strips lengthwise
Royal Icing:
  • 2 cups confectioners' sugar
  • 1 tablespoon meringue powder
  • 2 tablespoons black gel food coloring
  • 3 tablespoons water

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (176°C) for the cupcakes. Line two 12-cup muffin tins with orange paper liners.
  2. Melt the butter in a small skillet over medium heat; cook and swirl the pan occassionally until the butter browns(around 6-9 minutes). Immediately move the butter to a medium bowl and let cool slightly. Whisk in the eggs, granulated sugar, milk, and vanilla.
  3. Whisk the all-purpose and almond flour, baking powder, pumpkin pie spice, and salt in an large bowl. Add the brown butter mixture and whisk until just combined(do not overmix).
  4. Divide the batter among the prepared muffin cups and fill them three quarters of the way. Bake until the tops of the cupcakes sping back when gently pressed(around 18-22 minutes). Let the cupcakes cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then remove to a rack to cool completely.
  5. For the frosting: Beat the salt and butter until smooth and flurry(if using an electric mix, beat on medium-high speed). Reduce the speed to low and add confectioners' sugar, about 1 cup at a time. Increase the speed after each cup of confectioners' sugar added, beating until fluffy again, scraping down the sides of the bowl occassionally. Add the milk, vanilla, and orange foo coloring and beat until smooth.
  6. For the decorations: Pipe the frosting onto the cupcakes with a round pastry tip. Level the cupcake tops with an offset spatula to even and smooth layer. Use a toothpick or small skewer next to create "grooves" in the frosting to look like a pumpkin. Insert one of the chew chocolate quarter on the top of the cupcakes to look like the stem, and a draped sour gummy belt to look like the vine. Repeat for all remaining cupcakes and candy, then refrigerate untiil the frosting is slightly firm(around 15 minutes).
  7. For the royal icing: Whisk together the confectioners' sugar and meringue powder in a large bowl. Add 3 tablespos water and beat until soft, glossy peaks form(using a mixer on medium-hih speed), adding 1 more tablespoon water if necessary(should be thick, but pipable). add black gel food coloring until very black. Cover the bowl with a damp paper towel and plastic wrap to prevent icing from drying out until ready to use (Makes about 1 cup).
  8. Transfer the royal icing to a piping bag fitted with a sall round tip. Using the piping bag, pipe jack-o-latern faces on each cuppcake. Set aside until royal icing has set(around 30 minutes)
GHOSTLY GOODNESS

Haunted Ghost Brownies

Rich chocolate brownies decorated with cute chocolate ghosts and chocolate eyes.

⏱ 1 hour & 40 minutes

View Recipe

Ingredients

Brownies:
  • 1/2 cups(1 stick) salted butter
  • 3/4 cups granulated sugar
  • 1/4 cup dark brown sugar, packed
  • 2/3 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 2 eggs, room temperature
  • 1 egg yolk or 1/4 pumpkin puree(pumpkin puree will add a moist and velvety texture to brownines while keeping it fudgy)
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/4 unsweetened cocoa powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon expresso powder(optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cups all-purpose flour, sifted
  • Add-ins:1 cup semisweet chocolate chips(or replace with walnuts, almonds or other nuts)
Chocolate Ganache:
  • About 3/4 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1/2 heavy cream(add more as needed)
  • Small pinch of salt
White Chocolate Ghots:
  • 1/3 cup white chocolate chips
  • 2 teaspoons heavy cream
  • 3 drops white food coloring(optional)
Ghost Eyes and Mouth
  • 1 tablespoon semisweet or dark chocolate
  • 1/2 heavy cream
Toppings(optional)
  • Gold or white stars
  • Gold sanding sugar
  • Assorted sprinkles

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (176°C). Grease and line an 8 x 8" baking pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang for easy removal.
  2. In a saucepan over medium-low heat, stir together butter, granulated sugar, brown sugar, and chocolate chips until the butter is alost melted (small chunls are expected).
  3. Remove from heat and stir until smooth.(Some suagr granules will remain).
  4. Let the mixture cool for 5 minutes, then whisk the vanilla and eggs vigorously for 1 minute.
  5. Gently whisl in pumpkin puree, cocoa powder, salt, and expresso powder(if using) until just combined.
  6. Add flour and the mix with a large spoon or spatula until just combined.
  7. Gently fold in the add-ins, if using
  8. Pour the batter into the prepared pan, pushing batter to the corners and smooth the top over.
  9. Bake for 15 minutes,then lift the pan up around 3 inches in the oven before letting it drop on the over rack. This helps release air bubbles for a fudgier texture.
  10. Let the brownies bake for another 7-13 minutes (or around 19 minutes depending on oven). Brownies are done if the edges are set, there's cracking on the top. and a toothpick halfway between the edge and center comes out with a few moist crumbs(remember that chocolate chips make stick to the toothpicl).
  11. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan on the wire rack(around 2 hours).
  12. Moving onto the chocolate ganche: place the chocolate chips in a small heatproof bowl.
  13. Heat cream in a small spaucepan until steam rises from the suface, the pour it over the chocolate. Add a pinch of salt and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  14. Spread the ganache over the cooled brownies, tapping the pan on the counter afterwards to flatten the suface.
  15. Let set at a room temperature for around 30 minutes before decorating.
  16. Now for the white chocolate ghosts: line a baking sheet with parchment paper and a ghost template.
  17. Melt white chocolate chips and heavy cream in the microwave for 30 seconds or more as needed, stirring afterwards until smooth. Add white food coloring if using.
  18. Let the ganache cool slightly in the bowl if too liquidy to prevent spreading.
  19. Pipe or spoon 1 teaspoon of white chocolate onto the template. Use a toothpick to shape the chocolate into a ghost figure. Let them set for 20 minutes.
  20. For the ghost eyes and mouth: melt semisweet chocolate and heavy cream in the microwave for 30 seconds, stirring until smooth.
  21. Use a toothpick or piping bag to add eyes and mouths to the ghosts.
  22. Cool ghosts in the fridge for 30 minutes or until firm.
  23. Finally for the assembly: Carefully peel the ghosts off the parchment paper and place them on the ganche-covered brownies. Add stars and sprinkles for decoration.
  24. Chill for 2-3 hours in the fridge, remove from pan, then cut into 9-16 squares(or perferred number of squares).
  25. Allow the cut brownies to sit at room temperature for 30 minutes before serving.
MONSTER APPROVED

Monster Eye Cookies

Chewy cookies with colorful frosting, candy eyes, and plenty of Halloween personality.

⏱ 20 minutes

View Recipe

Ingredients

  • 1 box white cake mix
  • 1/2 cup, softened butter
  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 egg
  • Powder sugar
  • Different food coloring of bakers choice.
  • Candy eyes

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (176°C).
  2. Beat butter, vanilla, egg and cream cheese together until fluffy.
  3. Mix in the cake mix into the batter. Divide the batter afterwards into individual bowls for the amount of colors you want.
  4. Add food coloring to each individual bowl and mix until they are all combined. Then let it chill for 30 minutes.
  5. Roll into balls and dip in a bowl of powdered sugar.
  6. Place the balls on a greased cookie sheet and pat down a bit. Thenplace them to bake for 10-12 minutes..
  7. Take the cookies out from the oven and add the candy eyeballs while they're warm. Then let them cool down.
DARK & DELICIOUS

Spiderweb Cheesecake

A smooth, creamy baked cheesecake on an Oreo crust, topped with rich chocolate ganache and a piped white chocolate spiderweb.

⏱ 2 hours 45 minutes + 5 hours cooling & chilling

View Recipe

Ingredients

Crust:
  • 2 cups finely crushed Oreo cookies
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted
Cheesecake Filling:
  • 2 pounds (4 blocks / 32 oz) cream cheese, softened at room temperature
  • 1 1/3 cups granulated sugar
  • 4 large eggs, room temperature
  • 1 tablespoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 2/3 cup sour cream, room temperature
  • 2/3 cup heavy whipping cream, room temperature
Chocolate Ganache Topping:
  • 2 oz bittersweet or dark chocolate, finely chopped
  • 1/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons heavy whipping cream
Spiderweb Piping:
  • 1 to 2 oz good quality white chocolate chips or chopped block

Instructions

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Lightly grease a 9-inch springform pan with nonstick spray.
  2. In a bowl, mix together crushed Oreos and melted butter until completely coated. Firmly press into the bottom of the pan and about 1/2 inch up the sides. Bake for 12–15 minutes until set, then let cool on a wire rack.
  3. Lower the oven temperature to 325°F (162°C). Using a stand mixer fitted with a paddle, beat cream cheese until light and airy. Add sugar and continue beating on high for 3 minutes.
  4. Mix in vanilla extract and salt until incorporated. Add the eggs one at a time, beating gently and scraping down the sides after each addition. Mix in the sour cream followed by the heavy cream just until smooth.
  5. Securely wrap the outside bottom and sides of the springform pan with aluminum foil. Pour batter into the crust, leveling the surface. Set inside a high-sided roasting pan and fill the outer pan with hot water until it reaches halfway up the side of the cheesecake pan.
  6. Bake for 1.5 hours. Turn the oven off and prop the door open slightly (about 1 inch), leaving the cake inside for another hour. Remove from water bath, loosely tent with foil, and chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
  7. For the ganache: Place chopped bittersweet chocolate in a heatproof bowl. Scald heavy cream in a small saucepan over medium heat, then pour over the chocolate. Let sit for a minute, then whisk until glossy and combined. Melt white chocolate separately in 30-second microwave increments.
  8. Unmold the chilled cheesecake onto a platter. Spread the dark chocolate ganache smoothly across the top. Pipe white chocolate in concentric rings starting from the center outward. Draw a toothpick or wooden skewer from the center ring toward the outer rim repeatedly to create the web pattern. Chill until set before serving.
