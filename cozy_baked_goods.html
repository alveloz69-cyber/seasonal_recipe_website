AUTUMN CLASSIC

Spiced Apple Pie

A traditional autumn pastry featuring tart apple slices combined with concentrated cider syrup and fragrant warm spices inside a flaky butter crust.

⏱ 2 hours 30 minutes total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Crust Components:
  • 2 1/2 cups standard flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon granulated sugar
  • 1 cup cold unsalted butter, diced
  • 1 tablespoon apple cider vinegar
  • 1/2 cup ice water
Filling Components:
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground cloves
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • 3 1/2 pounds fresh pie apples, peeled and sliced
  • 1/3 cup boiled apple cider reduction
  • 1/2 cup white sugar & 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice
  • Egg wash and coarse crystal sugar

Instructions

  1. Blend flour, salt, and sugar. Cut in cold butter chunks until uniform crumbs form, then mix in vinegar and ice water until a dough forms. Divide in two, wrap, and chill for one hour.
  2. Mix cinnamon, cardamom, ginger, cloves, and nutmeg together in a compact bowl for your spice blend.
  3. Toss sliced apples with boiled cider, lemon juice, sugars, cornstarch, and a portion of your spice mixture in a large mixing bowl.
  4. Preheat your oven to 400°F (204°C). Roll out the first dough portion and press it into a 9-inch pie pan.
  5. Pile the coated fruit into the pastry base, cover with the second rolled dough sheet, trim the overlapping edges, crimp tightly, and slit ventilation vents on top.
  6. Brush the top crust lightly with egg wash and finish with a heavy dusting of crystal sugar.
  7. Bake at 400°F for 20 minutes, lower the heat to 375°F (190°C), and bake for another 40 to 50 minutes until bubbling and deeply browned. Let cool entirely before serving.
HARVEST BAKING

Spiced Pumpkin Loaf Cake

A moist harvest cake infused with autumn spices and crowned with a luscious layer of cream cheese spread.

⏱ 1 hour 15 minutes total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Loaf Ingredients:
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 7 tablespoons softened butter
  • 2 room temperature eggs
  • 1 1/4 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon & 1 tsp ginger
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup canned pumpkin purée
Cream Cheese Spread:
  • 1/2 cup cream cheese & 4 tbsp butter, softened
  • 1 1/2 cups powdered sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Instructions

  1. Set oven heat to 340°F (170°C) and line a standard loaf tin with baking parchment.
  2. Beat the butter and sugar with an electric mixer for 3 minutes until smooth and pale.
  3. Add eggs individually, mixing thoroughly after each addition.
  4. Whisk the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, and salt together in a separate container.
  5. Fold the dry mixture into the wet batter until combined, then gently blend in the pumpkin purée.
  6. Transfer the batter into your lined tin and smooth out the top with a spatula.
  7. Bake for 50–60 minutes until a wooden skewer inserted in the middle emerges clean. Rest in the pan for 10 minutes before moving to a wire rack.
  8. Whip the cream cheese and butter until fluffy, blend in the powdered sugar and vanilla, and spread across the cooled loaf.
MAPLE SWEETS

Chewy Maple Pecan Cookies

Soft-baked drop cookies flavored with genuine maple syrup, crunchy chopped pecans, and a light dusting of flaky sea salt.

⏱ 1 hour total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Cookie Ingredients:
  • 1/2 cup softened unsalted butter
  • 3/4 cup packed dark brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup pure maple syrup
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 3/4 cups standard flour
  • 1 3/4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 3/4 teaspoon fine salt
  • 1 1/2 cups chopped pecans
  • Flaky sea salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment paper.
  2. Cream the butter and brown sugar together on medium speed until fluffy and light.
  3. Mix in the egg, followed by the maple syrup and vanilla extract, beating until uniform.
  4. Combine the flour, baking powder, and fine salt in a medium bowl.
  5. Gradually blend the dry mix into your wet base, then fold through the chopped pecans.
  6. Scoop portioned mounds onto baking sheets, flatten tops slightly, and sprinkle with flaky sea salt.
  7. Bake for 12 to 16 minutes until edges are set while centers remain soft. Cool briefly on the trays before moving.
BREAKFAST TREATS

Cinnamon Roll Swirl Muffins

Bakery-style buttermilk muffins packed with cinnamon brown sugar ribbons, a buttery crumble topping, and cream cheese glaze.

⏱ 1 hour 35 minutes total

View Recipe

Ingredients

Components:
  • 3 cups flour, 3 tsp baking powder, 1/2 tsp baking soda, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup buttermilk, 1 cup sugar, 1/2 cup oil, 2 tsp vanilla, 2 eggs
  • Brown sugar and cinnamon swirl mix
  • Melted butter, flour, sugar, cinnamon, and salt streusel blend
  • Cream cheese, heavy cream, and powdered sugar glaze

Instructions

  1. Whisk flour, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Combine eggs, buttermilk, sugar, oil, and vanilla separately, then fold wet into dry. Rest the batter for 1 hour.
  2. Prepare the cinnamon sugar swirl and separately mix the chilled crumb streusel ingredients.
  3. Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C) and line a muffin tin with paper liners.
  4. Layer batter, cinnamon sugar, and extra batter into each cup, finishing with the crumb topping.
  5. Bake at 425°F for 5 minutes, lower heat to 350°F (175°C), and bake 18–20 minutes longer. Drizzle with cream cheese glaze once cooled.
